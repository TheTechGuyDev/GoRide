Delete your GoRide account

For the GoRide and GoRide Driver apps

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  1. Open GoRide or GoRide Driver and sign in.
  2. Tap Account at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Option 2: By email

Send an email from the address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my GoRide account". We will delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Email a deletion request

Deleted right away: your name, email, phone number, photo, date of birth, saved places, saved cards, emergency contacts, driver documents and notifications. You are signed out everywhere.

Kept without your name (up to 6 years): trip and payment records, because tax and accounting law requires them.

Any money left in your wallet is lost, so use or settle it first. Drivers with unpaid commission must settle it before deleting.
