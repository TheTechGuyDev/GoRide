Questions

How is my fare worked out?

A base fare, plus distance and time, with an airport fee only at airports and a small fee if the nearest driver is far away. Busy-time pricing is capped at 1.3×. You always see the full fare before you book.

Can I pay with cash?

Yes. You can pay cash, use your GoRide wallet, or pay with a card saved through Paystack.

How do I become a driver?

Tap "Sign up to drive", create your account and upload your documents. Once our team approves you, sign in to the GoRide Driver app with the same email and go online.

How much commission does GoRide take?

10% per trip, 7% on Power Driver days (20+ trips), and 0% for founding drivers in their first 30 days. Or choose a flat daily, weekly or monthly plan with no commission.

What if I lose something in the car?

Open the trip in your Activity tab and contact your driver, or reach our support team from Help in the app.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Account, then Delete my account. You can also request deletion here.
