Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

GoRide ("we", "us") runs the GoRide rider app, the GoRide Driver app and this website. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Data we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, gender and date of birth (optional), profile photo.
  • Location: riders: pickup and drop-off points and your location while booking and on a trip. Drivers: your live location while you are online, including when the app is in the background, so riders can be matched and follow the trip.
  • Trip records: routes, times, fares, tips, ratings and messages between rider and driver.
  • Payments: wallet history. Card payments are handled by Paystack. We only store the card type and last 4 digits, never the full card number.
  • Driver documents: driver's licence, vehicle papers, photos and bank details for payouts.
  • Safety: emergency contacts you add, and SOS alerts you send.
  • Device data: app version, device type and a notification token for push notifications.

2. How we use it

  • To match riders with nearby drivers, show live trips and calculate fares.
  • To process payments, payouts and commission.
  • To verify drivers and keep riders and drivers safe, including responding to SOS alerts.
  • To send trip updates, receipts and important account messages.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Who we share it with

  • The other person on your trip: riders see the driver's name, photo, car and live location; drivers see the rider's name, photo and pickup point.
  • Service providers: Supabase (secure database and hosting), Paystack (payments), Google (sign-in and notifications) and OpenStreetMap-based map services. They only process data on our instructions.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal details immediately. Trip and payment records are kept without your name for up to 6 years for accounting, tax and dispute purposes.

5. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, object to some uses, and ask for a copy. Most of this you can do in the app. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Delete your account: in the app go to Account > Delete my account, or follow the steps on our account deletion page.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by strict database rules, and secret keys are stored encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in email safe.

7. Children

GoRide is for people aged 18 and over. Riders under 18 must travel with an adult account holder.
